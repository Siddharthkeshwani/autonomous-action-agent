# Healthcare reschedule policy

## Check the appointment

- Confirm the booking reference, clinic, appointment time, and time zone.
- Offer one free reschedule when the request arrives at least 24 hours before the appointment.
- Within 24 hours, ask the clinic to confirm availability and any fee before making a change.
- If the clinic cancels, offer a new slot or review the prepaid amount for a full refund.

## Make the change

- Confirm the new slot and any fee with the customer.
- Get explicit approval before changing the booking or issuing a refund.
- Send the new booking reference and appointment time after the change succeeds.

## Case notes

- Record the booking reference, requested date, and clinic response.
- Keep the original booking reference with the new one for follow-up.
- Keep medical details out of general support notes.

# Subscription coin policy

## Coin balance

- Credit plan coins once the subscription payment settles.
- Coins can be spent on eligible audio content and expire 30 days after credit.
- After cancellation, unused coins remain available until their original expiry date.
- Coins stay with the account that earned them.

## Missing coins

- Check the payment status and the account's coin history.
- For a settled payment with missing coins, send the payment reference to Subscription Support.
- For failed or pending debits, follow the UPI dispute SLA.

## Refund review

- Verify how many coins were credited and spent before quoting a refund.
- Send cases with spent coins to Subscription Support for review.
- Get explicit approval before changing a balance or issuing a refund.

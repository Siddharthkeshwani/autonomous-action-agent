# UPI dispute SLA

## Open the case

- Ask for the transaction ID or UPI reference, bank, amount, and payment time.
- Check whether the payment is successful, failed, or pending.
- Record whether the customer's bank account was debited.
- Keep one dispute per payment reference.

## Follow up

- Acknowledge the dispute within one business day.
- For a failed AutoPay debit, check the reversal with Payments Ops before offering a retry.
- For a pending debit, check for settlement or reversal before retrying the payment.
- Escalate to Payments Ops if the debit remains unresolved after three business days.

## Close the case

- Confirm the reversal or settlement before marking the case resolved.
- Share the reference and next update time with the customer.

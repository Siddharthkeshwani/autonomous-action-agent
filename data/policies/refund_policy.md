# Refund policy

## Check the payment

- Confirm the order ID, the captured amount, and any earlier refund.
- For a pending or failed payment that debited the customer, follow the UPI dispute SLA.
- Send approved refunds to the original payment method.

## Check eligibility

- E-commerce returns must be requested within seven calendar days of delivery. Process the refund once the return passes inspection.
- Refund duplicate charges after confirming that both payments settled.
- For a cancelled healthcare appointment, check the reschedule policy before quoting a refund.
- For audio plans with spent coins, send the case to Subscription Support for review.

## Before issuing a refund

- Record the amount, order ID, and reason in the case.
- Get explicit approval before submitting the refund.
- Share the refund reference and quote five to seven business days after approval.
